AER web privacy & cookies

Updated 28 October 2026. This notice covers the AER browser app and its Telegram Mini App. The independent app owner is Viktor Danyshchuk, London Automation Solutions. Contact: victor.danyschuk@gmail.com.

Use AER without analytics

Breathing practices work without accepting optional analytics. Choose Manage, leave Usage analytics off and save your choice, or choose Without analytics. You can reopen Menu → Cookie preferences at any time. No account is required. The AI chat is disabled in this web edition.

Essential local storage

AER remembers language preferences and your cookie choice and stores app files for offline use. The cookie-choice record (aer.cookie-choice.v1) stays in this browser until you change it or clear browser data. A necessary first-party backup cookie (aer_cookie_choice_v1, up to one year, subject to browser limits) also remembers acceptance or refusal if local storage is unavailable. AER and YouCan keep separate choices; Safari and Telegram may use separate browser storage. Custom breathing rhythms and tile order are stored locally under aer.rhythms.v1 when you create or rearrange them. They stay in this browser until you remove them or clear browser data, without automatic sync to another device or the native app. Disabling optional analytics does not delete these settings. Custom rhythm names and entered values are not sent to usage analytics. This storage supports your requested settings and is separate from optional usage measurement.

Optional usage analytics

Only after permission, AER sets a random first-party browser cookie (aer_analytics_key, up to 180 days) and assigns a server-side browser number. We record public control labels when clicked, scroll milestones, approximate active time, page visits, approximate country, device type, operating system and browser category and referral source. We do not collect text entered in forms or chats, microphone recordings, precise GPS location, full referral URLs, Telegram profile information or advertising click IDs.

A browser number identifies a consenting browser installation, not a verified person. Another browser or the Telegram webview may get a different number. The owner can add a private label, such as My iPhone, when the browser is known to them. We do not fingerprint devices or join identities across apps.

Storage and Telegram notifications

Optional usage records are processed using Cloudflare Workers and D1. Country is derived from Cloudflare’s approximate network geolocation and can reflect a VPN, proxy or privacy relay rather than physical location. Visitor analytics does not store IP addresses or include them in Telegram notifications. Previously retained raw IPs have been removed from the active visitor database and stored delivery queue. Short-lived keyed hashes may be used for request rate limiting; they are not visitor analytics. Browser numbers, owner labels and saved cookie choices are preserved.

The app owner receives browser-consent notifications and grouped activity summaries in a private Telegram bot, and can request statistics there. This sends selected usage information to Telegram as a messaging provider. It is not a public activity feed. Server records expire after 90 days of inactivity; individual events expire after 90 days. Telegram messages already delivered can remain in the owner’s chat until deleted separately.

Change your choice or delete history

Open Menu → Cookie preferences and choose Without analytics to stop collection, remove the browser identifier and request deletion of its server-side history. If offline, the deletion request is retried on a later visit. This does not automatically remove messages already delivered to Telegram. To ask about those messages, access, correction or deletion, contact the owner using the email above and, if available, your browser number.

Hosting and third parties

GitHub Pages hosts the app. GitHub, Cloudflare and Telegram may process connection information needed to operate their services under their own policies. No advertising trackers are included. Launching AER from Telegram does not automatically give this analytics system your Telegram name or user ID.

Children and changes

Optional analytics is intended for adults. If you are under 18, use AER without optional analytics. If the purpose of analytics changes, we will ask again before enabling the changed collection.
