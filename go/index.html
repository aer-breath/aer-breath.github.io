A few things worth knowing.

Do I need to create an account?

You can start the web apps without an account. Open an app, choose a breathing practice or name a habit, and get going.

Can I use these on Android?

Yes. Both web apps work on Android in your browser. You can also add an icon to your home screen when your browser supports it. The native downloads linked here are for iPhone.

Will my habits and rhythms be saved?

The web apps save your habits, progress and custom rhythms in that browser on that device. They do not automatically sync with another browser, device or the iPhone app. Clearing browser data can remove that progress.

How does Telegram work?

The Telegram link opens our bot. Tap Start if asked, then choose the app button in its message to open the web app inside Telegram. The bot is not required to use the apps.
